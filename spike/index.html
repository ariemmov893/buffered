Buffered: Phase 0 spike

Throwaway measurement page. It shows what this browser receives from your controller and how finely it can time it. It applies no game rules and does no SOCD cleaning.

All timing on this page is approximate. A page normally reads the controller once per display frame, so anything that happens between two reads can be late by up to a frame or missed entirely. Keep this tab visible and focused while you test: browsers stop delivering controller data to background tabs.

0. Environment



    
1. What the controller sends

    

      Click this page, then press any button on the controller. Browsers hide controllers from a
      page until a button is pressed while the page has focus.
    

    

    


    
Mapping

    

      Take your hands off the controller, click Start, and follow the prompt. Use Skip for anything
      your controller does not have. Start again at any time to redo it.
    

    

      
      
      
    

    

    


    
2. Timing

    

      
      
    

    


    
Tap test

    

      Click Start and keep your hands off until the line below says GO. Then tap ONE button 20
      times, each tap as short as you can make it. Click Stop. Every input path that is active is
      measured at the same time, so the rows can be compared.

WebHID

    

      Nothing is requested until you click. The browser will show its own device chooser: pick the
